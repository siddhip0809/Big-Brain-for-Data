Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · click a node for detail.

People are not drawn as nodes — that made the graph unreadable. Instead each company's detail panel opens an org chart: the people we have mapped there, grouped by the department their title belongs to (or by location), most senior first, each expandable to their past employers. Every node in the 3D view is a company or a private backer, spread out in true 3D (not stacked rings). Companies loosely cluster and are colored by tier — Hyperscaler, NeoCloud/AI Infra, Developer/Operator, Cryptomining. Investors (violet) don't get a fixed home of their own — a company belongs to one tier, but an investor can back several at once, so each one sits wherever its actual backing pulls it: close to a single portfolio company, or toward the middle if it backs a spread of them.

Every claim carries its source links and three check flags — research team, Siddhi, Ward confirmed — click one to mark a claim as checked; the mark is saved for everyone who opens this page and pulled back into the brain.

Unconnected nodes (no line to anything shown) can be set apart — faded and pushed to an outer shell around their zone — or hidden altogether, from the switch under the legend.

Four adjacent-industry tiers — energy developers, general contractors, civil/land engineers, real estate & industrial developers — are in the brain from Siddhi's own lists but start switched off. Switch them on and they appear as their own separate zone beside the data-center core, never mixed into it; investors that back both stretch between the two. A company on more than one list shows as a banded node, one colour stripe per role (AECOM: civil + industrial + GC; TeraWulf: crypto pivot + neocloud).

In the core, solid company nodes are pure-play — data centers are the whole business, a direct-tap recruiting pool. Faded nodes are one arm of a bigger real estate, energy, telecom, or construction company (a Prologis, not a STACK) — check the detail panel before treating a candidate there as a straight DC hire.

Source: Clockwork CRM export, Siddhi's company lists, and web research.
