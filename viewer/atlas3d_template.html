Stepped back from the full graph on purpose: people are hidden for now so the shape of the industry is legible first. Every node here is a company or a private backer, spread out in true 3D (not stacked rings). Companies loosely cluster and are colored by tier — Hyperscaler, NeoCloud/AI Infra, Developer/Operator, Cryptomining. Investors (violet) don't get a fixed home of their own — a company belongs to one tier, but an investor can back several at once, so each one sits wherever its actual backing pulls it: close to a single portfolio company, or toward the middle if it backs a spread of them.

Four adjacent-industry tiers — energy developers, general contractors, civil/land engineers, industrial & cold-storage developers — are in the brain from Siddhi's own lists but start switched off; flip them on in the legend. A company on more than one list shows as a banded node, one colour stripe per role (AECOM: civil + industrial + GC; TeraWulf: crypto pivot + neocloud).

Solid company nodes are pure-play — data centers are the whole business, a direct-tap recruiting pool. Faded nodes are one arm of a bigger real estate, energy, telecom, or construction company (a Prologis, not a STACK) — check the detail panel before treating a candidate there as a straight DC hire.

Source: Clockwork CRM export + web research, 2026-09-05.
